# 01 — Galaxy Level: Structure & Star Distribution

Determines *where stars are*, *how many*, and the environmental context (age, metallicity) each star system inherits. This level makes the night sky from any system correct: the Milky Way band, local star density, and neighbor stars all come from the same model.

## Model

A single spiral galaxy defined analytically (density as a pure function of position), sampled lazily through a sector grid.

### Density model (stars per pc³)

Sum of components, each a closed-form function of galactocentric cylindrical coords `(R, z, φ)`:

- **Thin disk**: double-exponential `ρ ∝ exp(−R/h_R) · exp(−|z|/h_z)` with scale length `h_R ≈ 2.6 kpc`, scale height `h_z ≈ 300 pc`. Hosts most stars; younger population.
- **Thick disk**: same form, `h_z ≈ 900 pc`, ~10% local normalization; old, metal-poor population.
- **Bulge**: Sérsic-like spheroid within ~3 kpc; oldest stars, high density.
- **Stellar halo**: shallow power-law `ρ ∝ r^−3.5`, sparse, very metal-poor; also hosts globular clusters (placed as discrete seeded objects).
- **Spiral arms**: logarithmic spirals `φ(R) = ln(R/R₀)/tan(p)` with pitch angle `p ≈ 12°`, 2–4 arms. Arms are a *multiplicative* density enhancement (×2–3 for young stars, weak for old stars) plus the site of O/B associations and H II regions — arms modulate the *young* population strongly, matching reality.
- Normalization anchored to the observed solar-neighborhood density ≈ 0.1 stars/pc³ at `R = 8 kpc, z = 20 pc`.

### Population gradients

Each sampled star inherits from its birthplace:

- **Metallicity [Fe/H]**: radial gradient ≈ −0.06 dex/kpc from a bulge-region peak, plus component offsets (thick disk ≈ −0.6, halo ≈ −1.5) and per-star scatter (σ ≈ 0.2 dex). Feeds directly into planet occurrence (03).
- **Age distribution**: per component — thin disk uniform-ish over 0–10 Gyr weighted to a declining star-formation history, thick disk 8–12 Gyr, halo/bulge 10–13 Gyr. Age × mass drives evolutionary stage (02).
- **Kinematics**: circular velocity ≈ 220 km/s with component-dependent velocity dispersion — used only for star-field proper-motion flavor and binary population statistics, not integrated.

## Sector grid (lazy sampling)

- Space partitioned into cubic sectors (~10 pc for the local scale, hierarchical octave sectors above for far-field rendering aggregates).
- Per sector: expected star count `N = ρ(center) · V`; actual count drawn Poisson from the sector seed; star positions low-discrepancy within the sector, density-gradient-corrected.
- Each star slot gets: derived seed, position, population tag (component), [Fe/H], age → handed to the **star generator (02)** for everything else.
- Sectors are pure functions of `(universeSeed, sectorCoords)` — any sector can be generated independently, in any order, on any worker.

### Special structures (discrete, seeded placements)

- **Open clusters** along arms (co-moving, co-eval groups of 100–1,000 stars sharing age/metallicity)
- **Globular clusters** in the halo (10⁴–10⁶ stars; rendered as aggregate + resolved core when approached)
- **OB associations** and **emission nebulae** in arms (young hot stars + emission-spectrum gas glow)
- **Galactic center**: supermassive black hole + dense nuclear cluster

## Visual deliverables at this level

- **Procedural skybox from the model itself**: from any position, the background starfield is generated by sampling the density model — bright near stars individually (correct color from 02, magnitude from luminosity/distance via `m = M + 5·log₁₀(d/10pc)`), plus an aggregate unresolved glow for the Milky Way band computed by line-of-sight integration through the density model, dust extinction lane included (simple exponential dust disk, reddening applied spectrally through the 00 color pipeline).
- Nebulae as volumetric-look billboard clusters with emission-line palettes (Hα red, O III teal).
- Star colors in the field are **never artistic**: temperature → chromaticity via the 00 pipeline.

## Fidelity tiers

1. **v1**: analytic density + sectors + correct skybox from local sampling; single galaxy; clusters/nebulae as sprinkled placements.
2. **v2**: dust extinction integral, globular/open clusters resolvable, galactic-center region.
3. **Later**: multiple galaxies as distant objects (pure visuals), Magellanic-like satellites.

## Testing targets

- Local density normalization within tolerance at the solar radius.
- Sampled metallicity/age distributions match component definitions.
- Skybox regression: star counts per magnitude bin near a Sun-like location roughly match real all-sky counts (e.g. ~9,000 stars brighter than m=6.5).
